DT Platform4 — the WhatsApp Message Management System

Capture, store, manage, reply and audit — a single platform that turns WhatsApp from a risky, personal-phone habit into dependable business infrastructure.

What it is

Your WhatsApp inbox, rebuilt as a business system

DT Platform4 sits between Meta's WhatsApp Cloud API and your team. It receives every customer message through a verified webhook, permanently records it in MySQL, notifies your team through Mattermost, and lets staff reply from a purpose-built dashboard — with full delivery tracking on every message that goes out.

Nothing lives only on a phone. Nothing depends on one person's memory. Every conversation is a permanent, searchable, attributable business record.

How it works

From customer message to staff reply, in five reliable steps

1

Customer sends a WhatsApp message

A customer messages your business number through standard WhatsApp — no app installs, no new habits for them to learn.

2

Webhook receives & verifies it

Our PHP webhook validates the Meta signature, checks the request, and prevents duplicate processing before anything else happens.

3

Save to database — always first

The customer, conversation and message are written to MySQL immediately. This "Write First" principle means no message is ever lost, even if a later step fails.

4

Notify & automate

n8n and Mattermost are notified in parallel — triggering alerts, automations, or AI replies as configured for your business.

5

Staff reply, from anywhere

Your team replies from the dashboard or Mattermost — the reply is saved to the database first, then sent via the WhatsApp Cloud API, with delivery status tracked end-to-end.
