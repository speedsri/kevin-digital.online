Home / Features

Everything your team needs to run WhatsApp like a real business channel

Eight capabilities, working together, so no message, status, or staff member ever falls through the cracks.

Secure Staff Login

Password-hashed authentication with secure session management, so only authorized staff ever touch a customer conversation.

Chronological Message History

Every conversation is stored in full order — inbound and outbound — so any staff member has complete context instantly.

Dashboard Replies with User Tracking

Replies sent from the dashboard are stamped with the logged-in staff member's ID — accurate attribution, every time.

Mattermost Integration & Sender Mapping

Mattermost usernames map to real staff names via the sender-mapping table — raw usernames are always preserved for audit.

Auto-Reply Support

Automated and AI replies flow through the same "write first, then send" pipeline, tagged with an AUTO channel for transparency.

Media Handling

Images, PDFs and documents are captured and linked to the right conversation, ready to view straight from the dashboard.

Real-Time Status Tracking

Sent, delivered, read and failed states are captured from Meta's status webhooks for a complete delivery lifecycle.

Mobile-Responsive Dashboard

A familiar, chat-style interface that works beautifully whether your team is at a desk or replying from a phone.

Message origin, always visible

Know exactly where every reply came from

Each message carries a channel tag, so your reports and your team always know whether a reply came from a human on the dashboard, a staff member in Mattermost, or an automation.
