Who we are

A full-stack IT partner, specialized in WhatsApp business automation

Kevin Digital Developer provides complete IT and digital solutions — from servers and networking to security and business automation. Our core specialty is custom WhatsApp communication platforms: systems that capture, store, manage and route business messaging at scale, without ever compromising on reliability or data integrity.

We designed and built DT Platform4, our flagship WhatsApp Message Management System, from the ground up — combining the Meta WhatsApp Cloud API, a hardened PHP webhook layer, a normalized MySQL database, n8n automation, and Mattermost team collaboration into one coherent product.
